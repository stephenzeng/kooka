# Power BI
**Date created:** 2026-09-01 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Get Started, Guidance, Licensing  

## Moderate Changes

- **Copilot for Power BI overview**

  Updated guidance to streamline prerequisites and point to a consolidated “Requirements at a glance,” removing redundant details while keeping core requirements. Clarified data preparation by emphasizing semantic model readiness before using Copilot and trimmed duplicative notes while retaining the preview status. These refinements make setup clearer and reduce confusion for administrators and authors.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/copilot-introduction

- **Interact with reports and dashboards**

  Expanded end-user instructions for Reading view and consolidated Reading vs. Editing view with clearer permission behavior. Introduced an “Interactions at a glance” hub linking to common tasks, and refined steps, tips on licensing/admin controls, and device guidance. The article now focuses on practical interaction techniques by removing unrelated overview content.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/explore-reports/end-user-reading-view

- **Email subscriptions for reports and dashboards in the Power BI service**

  Added “Run now limits” detailing per-user, per-hour limits for instant subscription emails by license or capacity tier, with clear fallback to a default limit when the tier can’t be resolved. Documented the user-facing error when limits are reached and noted applicability to both Power BI and paginated reports. This helps admins and users plan subscriptions and avoid unexpected throttling.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/end-user-subscribe
