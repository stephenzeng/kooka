**Change date:** 2026-08-07 UTC  
**Tags:** AI, Analytics  

## Moderate Changes

- **Create a narrative visual with Copilot for Power BI**
  
  Updated guidance on creating the narrative visual and added an option to include hidden bookmark visuals, with steps for selecting them in the UI. Clarified key limitations: only display-only bookmarks (no Data state) are supported, bookmarks must be accessible via a button or navigator, personal bookmarks aren’t supported, and visuals on hidden pages are excluded unless revealed by a report bookmark. This helps report authors configure narratives reliably and avoid unsupported configurations.
  
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/copilot-create-narrative
