# Microsoft 365 Copilot
**Date created:** 2026-08-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Billing, Consumption, Guidance  

## New Articles

- **View Copilot Credit consumption in the Microsoft 365 admin center and on your Azure bill**

  Introduced guidance for viewing and reconciling Copilot Credit usage between the Microsoft 365 admin center and Azure Cost Management and billing. Clarifies how operational usage differs from billing data, including actual versus amortized costs and invoice details, to reduce reconciliation friction. Outlines purchasing options and credit sources (capacity packs, Copilot Credit pre-purchase plans P3, Microsoft Agent P3, and pay-as-you-go) and explains the priority order in which credits are consumed. Provides FAQs on billing line items, matching credits to currency, and how task credit costs are calculated, with links to related management and billing pages.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/usage-based-billing-compare-dashboard-views

## Moderate Changes

- **Choose a model for Copilot Cowork**

  Updated model selection guidance: renamed “Best for” to “Description,” added Opus 5 for complex, high-stakes work, and removed Claude Opus 4.8 and the “Sonnet + Opus Advisor” paired mode. Reordered models and refined descriptions for GPT 5.5 (Frontier), GPT 5.6 Sol, and GPT 5.6 Terra, and removed obsolete references to “Sonnet + Opus Advisor” status indicators to reflect current capabilities and improve clarity.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-models
