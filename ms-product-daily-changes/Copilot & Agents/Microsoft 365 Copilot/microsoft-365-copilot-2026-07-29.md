# Microsoft 365 Copilot
**Date created:** 2026-07-29 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Agent, AI, Governance  

## Major Changes

- **Customize the Employee Self-Service agent**

  Added a new “Stay up to date” capability that surfaces configurable personalized update cards on the agent landing page, including status, actions, summaries, timestamps, and quick actions. Clarified what employees will see, the types of updates shown, and prerequisites such as adding the agent and configuring at least one connector (for example, ServiceNow) with ticket-related topics. Updated admin setup guidance to use Microsoft 365 admin center > Copilot > Settings > Data access > View all, and enable the feature under the Personalized content tab with the “Show stay up to date cards” toggle. Expanded rich landing page guidance and clarified default behavior when the feature isn’t configured, helping admins roll out the experience confidently.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/employee-self-service/customize

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot Personal Content Mode**

  Announced deprecation of Personal Content Mode, scheduled for removal in August 2026. Advised customers to update any dependent workflows or experiences to avoid disruptions ahead of retirement.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/microsoft-365-copilot-personal-content-mode
