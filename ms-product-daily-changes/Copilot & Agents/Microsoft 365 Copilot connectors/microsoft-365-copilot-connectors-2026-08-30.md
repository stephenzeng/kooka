## Major Changes

- **Submit a federated Microsoft 365 Copilot connector**

  Expanded and restructured the article to provide end-to-end guidance for submitting federated connectors. Added detailed coverage of connector capabilities across Copilot surfaces, domain-based scenarios, and distinct publishing paths for ISVs and line-of-business teams. Introduced step-by-step packaging and submission via Partner Center with prerequisites, pre-certification testing, sample prompt guidance, and cross-surface validation. Added options for LOB publishing through the Microsoft 365 admin center, a sideloading test procedure with security notes, and clarified misconceptions (connectors vs. plugins, surface coverage, store vs. admin center). Refined required metadata and assets, consolidated review and onboarding expectations, and removed outdated gallery requirements to streamline submission.
