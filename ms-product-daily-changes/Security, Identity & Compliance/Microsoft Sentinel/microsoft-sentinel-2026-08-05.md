## Moderate Changes

- **Microsoft Defender portal implementation guide for Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs)**

  Updated guidance on Microsoft Entra B2B limitations in Defender for Office 365, narrowing the listed restrictions to releasing email from quarantine and removing references to managing spam/phishing policies, TABL, and Threat Explorer. Expanded the Email and collaboration tools section to clearly differentiate capabilities when using Microsoft Entra B2B versus GDAP, noting that B2B constraints stem from Exchange Online RBAC. Added pointers to GDAP-supported workloads and least-privileged role guidance to help MSSPs choose the right access model.
