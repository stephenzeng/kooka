## Moderate Changes

- **Audit log activities**

  Updated the Audit log activities article to include new Viva Glint-to-Insights integration events: GlintToInsightsIntegrationEnabled, GlintToInsightsIntegrationDisabled, and GlintToInsightsIntegrationUpdated. These additions help administrators audit when the integration is enabled or disabled and when its configuration changes, improving traceability for compliance and troubleshooting. No other sections were changed.
