# Microsoft Purview
**Date created:** 2026-09-02 UTC  
**Tags:** Compliance, Deprecation, Monitoring, Security  

## Major Changes

- **Investigate Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management activities**
  Announced deprecation of the Data risk graph in Insider Risk Management, with retirement on November 24, 2026. After this date, the experience will no longer be available, and related visuals were removed from the article. Customers should plan for the feature’s removal and update any documentation or training that references it.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/insider-risk-management-activities

- **Data risk graph in Insider Risk Management**
  Introduced an IMPORTANT deprecation notice for the Data risk graph, which will be retired on November 24, 2026. The experience will not be accessible after that date. Organizations should review dependencies and prepare for the change.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/insider-risk-management-data-risk-graph

- **Learn about Microsoft Sentinel in Microsoft Purview**
  Added an IMPORTANT notice that the Data risk graph in Insider Risk Management will be retired on November 24, 2026. The experience will be unavailable after that date. Readers are advised to account for this change in their monitoring and reporting workflows.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/purview-sentinel

## Moderate Changes

- **Audit log activities**
  Added coverage for Microsoft Defender XDR case management activities in the Microsoft 365 audit log, including operations like creating, updating, linking, and closing cases. This helps administrators identify and interpret case lifecycle events during investigations and compliance reviews.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/audit-log-activities
