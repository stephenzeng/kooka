# Microsoft Defender for Office 365
**Date created:** 2026-08-21 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Configuration, Guidance, Security, Troubleshooting  

## Major Changes

- **Simulation automations for Attack simulation training**

  Expanded guidance clarifies core concepts and execution flow, including distinctions between automations, runs, and delivery batches, plus region-aware delivery and randomized batching behavior. Scheduling guidance was significantly revised, contrasting Randomized vs. Fixed schedules, explaining that randomized maximums are upper bounds, and outlining factors that can reduce run counts. The setup experience now has clearer details for maximum runs, randomized send times, unique payloads per run, and user targeting, supported by a settings interaction table. New sections help predict automation outcomes and provide troubleshooting for common issues, and the FAQ adds practical clarifications to avoid misconfiguration. These updates help admins plan realistic simulations, ensure correct targeting and cadence, and reduce surprises in execution.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/attack-simulation-training-simulation-automations

## Moderate Changes

- **View Defender for Office 365 reports in the Microsoft Defender portal**

  Removed the Preview note for the Protection & posture insights report, indicating the feature is now generally available. Customers can rely on stable behavior and guidance without preview caveats.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/reports-defender-for-office-365
