**Date created:** 2026-08-06 UTC

## New Articles

- **Column visualizations for grids in Power Apps**
  
  Introduced a new how-to article on adding custom column visualizations to model-driven app grids, including Radial Dial, Line Chart, Heat Map, and Star Rating. Provides step-by-step setup guidance, data format requirements, and best practices to ensure accurate rendering. Includes data preparation tips and a troubleshooting table for common display issues. The feature is in preview and includes illustrative examples to help makers choose the right visualization for their scenario.
  
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/data-platform/column-visualizations-grids

## Moderate Changes

- **Frequently asked questions when transitioning from legacy data integration services to Fabric link and Azure Synapse Link for Dataverse**
  
  Updated the FAQ to extend the retirement date for Azure Synapse Analytics trusted services from August 1, 2026 to August 1, 2027. Encourages organizations to use the additional time to review network configurations and plan transitions to supported options such as private endpoints and managed virtual networks to maintain secure, compliant connectivity.
  
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/data-platform/azure-synapse-link-transition-FAQ

- **Common issues and resolutions for solution checker**
  
  Expanded troubleshooting for analyses that remain “running” by noting that blocking .zip attachments or the application/zip MIME type can prevent result delivery. Adds a reference to manage privacy and security settings and reiterates that .NET Standard 2.0 plugin components are unsupported and should be excluded to avoid stalled checks.
  
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/data-platform/common-issues-resolutions-solution-checker
