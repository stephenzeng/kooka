# Power Apps
**Date created:** 2026-07-29 UTC  
**Tags:** Other  

## Major Changes

- **Create a canvas app with data from a list**

  Updated guidance to introduce a new single-screen, responsive app template for apps generated from SharePoint/Lists. The template consolidates browse, details, and create/edit/delete into one container-based screen that adapts to tablet and phone, simplifying layout and maintenance. Added a note clarifying the shift from the previous three-screen, phone-oriented template and that existing generated apps are unaffected. Removed outdated descriptions and the prior instructional video reference to align with the new experience.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/canvas-apps/app-from-sharepoint
