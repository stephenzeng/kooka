# Power Apps
**Date created:** 2026-07-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Major Changes

- **Release channel for your model-driven app**

  Updated guidance clarifies that semi-annual is the default channel and that monthly and semi-annual deliver the same GA features, with timing differences. Added clear explanations of how channels relate, when features appear in each, and how to verify the current build from the app’s About dialog. Reworked the monthly schedule to describe weekly, region-grouped rollout, build naming (YYMM.W), and notification timing, and linked to GA deployment documentation. Introduced a new semi-annual schedule section covering rollout cadence (early April/October), typical build identifiers, and alignment with release plans, while removing outdated region tables and examples. These changes help admins plan deployments, set expectations for feature availability, and align environments with organizational release strategies.
