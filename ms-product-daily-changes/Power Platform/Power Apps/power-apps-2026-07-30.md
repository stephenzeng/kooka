## New Articles

- **Configure sensitivity labels for emails**

  Introduced end-to-end guidance for adding and configuring sensitivity labels on email forms in Power Apps. Explains how to enable labels, set default behaviors, and ensure policies are applied consistently. This helps organizations protect sensitive information and meet compliance requirements in email workflows. The article consolidates steps via a shared include to streamline setup and administration.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/user/configure-email-sensitivity

- **Use sensitivity labels in emails**

  Added instructions for applying sensitivity labels while composing emails to classify and protect content. Covers how labels affect protection behaviors and provides practical usage guidance to promote consistent data governance. This helps users reduce accidental data exposure and align with organizational compliance policies, with step-by-step details provided through a shared include.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/user/use-sensitivity-labels

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure a row summary for a model-driven app**

  Added a Known limitations section clarifying constraints for row summaries, including limited support for date-based operations, potential hyperlink rendering issues, and possible inaccuracies with formula and calculated columns. Also notes limitations interpreting metadata (such as choice labels and related-table associations) and the lack of support for rich formatting, citations, and footnotes to set clear expectations for use.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/data-platform/configure-form-row-summary
