## Moderate Changes

- **Create custom help pages for model-driven apps**

  Updated guidance on enabling custom help panes in model-driven apps, adding explicit navigation steps through Manage > environment > Settings > Product > Features. Clarified the setting name ("Custom help for customizable entities") and its mutual exclusivity with "Enable Custom Help Panes." This reduces setup errors and ensures admins configure help correctly.
