# Power Platform
**Date created:** 2026-07-25 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Frequently asked questions**

  Updated the FAQ to state that Unified Sandbox Environments (USE) and Unified Developer Environments (UDE) cannot be converted between each other. Administrators must provision a new environment of the desired type, helping avoid delays and rework during environment planning.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/developer/unified-experience/finance-operations-faq

- **Unified environment types and templates for finance and operations apps**

  Added a clarification in the UDE section that USE and UDE cannot be changed from one to the other after provisioning. This guidance helps admins choose the correct environment up front and reduces misconfiguration and migration effort.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/unified-experience/unified-environment-types-and-templates
