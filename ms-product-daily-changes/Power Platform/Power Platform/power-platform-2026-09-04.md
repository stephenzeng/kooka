## New Articles

- **Automate procurement order acknowledgment matching with Copilot Studio and SAP**

  Introduced a reference architecture for automating procurement order acknowledgment matching using a multi-agent solution in Copilot Studio integrated with SAP. The guidance walks through intake with Power Automate, secure storage in Dataverse, orchestration and field extraction, SAP purchase order retrieval, tolerance-based matching, automatic confirmations, and exception handling via Microsoft Teams with full auditing. It details component roles and end-to-end workflow, and provides deep considerations across reliability, security, operations, performance, experience, and responsible AI to support enterprise adoption.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/architecture/reference-architectures/order-acknowledgment-validation
