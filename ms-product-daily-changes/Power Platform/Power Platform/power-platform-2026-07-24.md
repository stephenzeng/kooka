# Power Platform
**Date created:** 2026-07-24 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Business continuity and disaster recovery**

  Updated cost and billing guidance for self-service disaster recovery, removing references to automatic pay-as-you-go charges. Clarified that secondary-region storage uses existing free storage and Dataverse entitlements, with the example noting the secondary 10 GB is drawn from those entitlements. Helps admins plan DR capacity without expecting separate prepaid storage.
