## New Articles

- **Troubleshoot Common Portal Management App Issues**

  Introduced a new troubleshooting guide for Power Pages Portal Management and Power Pages Management apps. Clarifies how to identify the correct data model (standard adx_ vs enhanced mspp_) and provides a step-by-step diagnostics workflow. Provides fixes for missing sites or app visibility, script errors or stuck loading (including extension conflicts), role requirements, and package update issues. Explains app access and web role assignments across both models, including mspp_webrole virtual table constraints, and outlines how to enable languages and surface translations. Includes best practices for configuring basic forms, multistep forms, and lists, plus what information to gather for Microsoft support.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/power-platform/power-pages/troubleshoot-portal-management-app
