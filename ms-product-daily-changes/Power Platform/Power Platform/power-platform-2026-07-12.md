## Moderate Changes

- **Auto-claim policies for Power Apps licenses**

  Updated guidance to clarify that auto-claim policies are not supported in US Sovereign Clouds (GCC, GCC High, and DoD). This helps admins avoid unsupported configurations in regulated environments; no functional behavior changed, but cloud availability is now explicitly documented.
