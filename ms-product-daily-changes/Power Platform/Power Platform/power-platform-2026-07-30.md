# Power Platform
**Date created:** 2026-07-30 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Security  

## New Articles

- **Configure sensitivity labels for emails**

  Introduced a new admin overview for enabling and managing sensitivity labels on emails in Power Platform. The article centralizes setup steps and governance considerations, helping organizations enforce data protection and consistent classification. It explains how to turn on label support and manage ongoing operations using shared, reusable guidance for easier maintenance and alignment with security policies.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/configure-email-sensitivity

## Moderate Changes

- **Licensing**

  Expanded end-user experience guidance with a new section that explains in-app license notification states (Informational, Warning, Error) and the 7-day escalation path until a valid license is obtained. Clarified which licenses include managed environments to reduce confusion, with examples and visuals to help administrators and support teams communicate next steps.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/managed-environment-licensing

- **Microsoft Azure VNet support overview**

  Standardized terminology to “VNet” and clarified region and failover guidance, including aligning VNet regions with environment regions and the use of VNet peering. Added operational steps with PowerShell cmdlets for subnet injection changes and DNS updates, and recommended using a NAT gateway for controlled outbound internet access, improving consistency and implementation clarity.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/vnet-support-overview
