# Power Platform
**Date created:** 2026-07-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Automation, Governance  

## Major Changes

- **Important changes (deprecations) coming in Power Platform**

  Announced the deprecation of the Power Automate mobile app for iOS and Android effective August 31, 2026. The change removes the app from stores and ends updates, support, push notifications, and widgets, while flows and Approvals continue to function through other channels. Updated guidance directs users, makers, and IT admins to transition to alternatives such as the Teams Approvals app, the Power Automate web portal, and Power Apps mobile, with noted limitations to consider. Organizations should plan migrations, communicate impacts, and adjust governance to ensure continuity.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming
