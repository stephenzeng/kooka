# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-07-15 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Analytics, Monitoring  

## Moderate Changes

- **Manage updates for Teams android devices in Pro Management portal (PMP)**

  Updated the default PMP update ring mappings, extending each duration range by one day (Validation→Staging: 0–6 days, General→General: 7–13 days, Final→Executive: 14–20 days). This gives admins a slightly longer buffer for staged rollouts, reducing risk and improving predictability.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/androidupdatemanagementinpmp

- **Prepare your organization's network for Microsoft Teams**

  Added guidance on the Best practice configurations dashboard for Teams meetings, explaining its purpose and how it highlights non-compliant locations. This helps admins prioritize remediation steps and align configurations with Microsoft recommendations to improve meeting quality.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/prepare-network

- **Administrative units for Microsoft Teams**

  Clarified that scoped administrators cannot manage Auto Attendants, Call Queues, or Resource Accounts. This sets accurate expectations for delegated admin roles and aids in planning support processes.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-administrative-units

- **Manage channel policies in Microsoft Teams**

  Added instructions for accessing Teams policies in the new admin center experience and referenced unified policies and settings. Replaced an include with explicit steps for assigning policies to users individually, in bulk, or via groups, streamlining policy deployment.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-policies

- **Microsoft Teams analytics and reporting**

  Added the Teams events usage report to the reporting reference, including availability (Admin center only; no Graph/API/Export) and detailed fields such as Event ID, title, organizers, access type, attendees, registration, recording, and RTMP. This enables administrators to understand what event insights are available and how to use them for analysis.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-analytics-and-reports/teams-reporting-reference

- **New VDI solution for Teams**

  Updated VDI optimization status for Omnissa Horizon to General Availability and confirmed the required Horizon client version (8.17 (2512) or higher). Adjusted Amazon WorkSpaces guidance to remove the GA mention while keeping the required client version (5.31.0.5733 or higher), clarifying supported environments and prerequisites for optimized performance.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/vdi-2
