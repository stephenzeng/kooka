# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-07-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Governance, Monitoring  

## Major Changes

- **Certified Teams panels**

  Expanded certified panels catalog by adding a new Cisco Navigator firmware entry and introducing Q-SYS as a new vendor. The Q-SYS Room Scheduling Panel QSP 11 now appears with current firmware and included Microsoft apps, broadening deployment options. These updates help organizations standardize room scheduling hardware with clearer versioning and support guidance, improving planning and lifecycle management.  

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/teams-panels-certified-hardware

## Moderate Changes

- **AI Archives for Microsoft Teams Meetings**

  Clarified that AI archives are stored in Teams-owned SharePoint Embedded containers and that Microsoft Purview retention policies govern these archives and override Teams Admin Center expiration settings. Guidance also explains that turning off AI archive generation does not disable existing Teams features but may reduce Copilot/Facilitator response quality and duration, and that archives persist when an organizer is deactivated unless retention policies dictate otherwise.  

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/ai-archive-teams-meetings

- **Teams Rooms on Android certified devices**

  Updated certified device listings with new July 27, 2026 firmware and app versions for multiple Cisco Android-based Teams devices. This ensures admins can align deployments with the latest supported builds for improved stability, security, and compatibility.  

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-hardware-android

- **Health reports in the Teams Management Pro portal**

  Moved Teams Rooms Health reporting to a new platform, shifting metrics to be room-based and now including offline tickets in health calculations. The Active Tickets trend chart is temporarily unavailable, and licensing guidance was clarified to cover Teams Rooms Pro and Teams Shared Space.  

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/health-reports

- **Release notes for Microsoft Teams Rooms**

  Added the July 2026 release entry highlighting fixes for device log collection reliability, enhanced diagnostic logging, displays turning off during Town hall/webinar, and a crash when ending a call. These improvements strengthen operational stability and simplify troubleshooting for room deployments.  

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/rooms-release-note
