# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-08-22 UTC  
**Tags:** Best Practices, Configuration, Get Started, Guidance, Licensing  

## Major Changes

- **Plan Direct Routing**

  Overhauled the planning guide with clearer scope, deployment overview, onboarding steps, and reasons to choose Direct Routing. Added a detailed architecture section and comprehensive infrastructure requirements with tables for SBCs, domains/DNS, certificates, connection points, firewall rules, and media transport. Clarified licensing across environments, including interactions with Audio Conferencing and guidance for GCC High/DoD. Expanded SIP signaling and media guidance with environment-specific FQDNs and IP ranges, required ports, failover behavior, supported codecs, and media processor geography. Introduced best practices, a deployment verification checklist, and hybrid patterns combining Calling Plan/Operator Connect with Direct Routing to streamline planning and rollout.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-plan

- **Plan for Teams Phone Mobile**

  Rewrote planning guidance with clear criteria for when to use Teams Phone Mobile and how the solution works end-to-end. Added a step-by-step planning framework covering operator selection, licensing and enablement, number assignment, emergency calling, and calling settings. Highlighted key capabilities such as a single business number, call continuity, an integrated Teams experience, and enhanced security and compliance. Clarified that only user phone numbers are supported (not Audio Conferencing or voice application numbers) and added next steps with links to PSTN connectivity and emergency calling resources.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/operator-connect-mobile-plan
