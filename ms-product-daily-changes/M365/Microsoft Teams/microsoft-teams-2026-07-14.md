# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-07-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Teams Rooms certified systems and peripherals**

  Added a new certified device: Owl Labs Meeting Owl 5 Pro. Removed several Poly cameras and introduced an End of Service/Life section to clearly identify EOS/EOL devices, helping admins plan device refresh and support strategies.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/certified-hardware

- **Teams Rooms on Android certified devices**

  Added certified entries for Poly TC8 (HP Direct Connect) Virtual Touch Console and Poly TC10 (HP Direct Connect) Virtual Touch Console at version 4.7.0, with certification ending September 3, 2026. Noted compatibility with Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, and G7500 to aid procurement and deployment planning.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-hardware-android
