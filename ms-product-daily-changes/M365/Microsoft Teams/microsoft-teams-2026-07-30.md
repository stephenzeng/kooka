# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-07-30 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## New Articles

- **Enroll and provision Teams Android devices in the Pro Management portal**

  Introduced an end-to-end guide for enrolling and provisioning Teams Android devices using the Teams Rooms Pro Management portal. Covers prerequisites, supported device types and identifiers, and a step-by-step workflow for adding devices individually or via CSV, generating time-limited verification codes, and completing on-site activation or remote sign-in. Details status values, device management actions (edit/delete), code lifecycle, and troubleshooting for common errors and permissions. Notes current limitations (for example, no bulk remote sign-in) and clarifies the shift from the previous TAC experience to the Pro Management portal.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/provisionandroiddevicespromanagementportal

## Moderate Changes

- **Setup - Direct dial Microsoft Copilot Studio voice agents through Microsoft Teams Phone**

  Updated applicability to focus solely on Microsoft Teams by removing Skype for Business from supported products. This clarifies deployment scope and reduces confusion for administrators managing Teams Phone integrations.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-direct-mcs

- **Release Notes for Teams Android Devices Management Apps**

  Added support for Authenticator version 6.2606.4246 (July 2026) and documented improvements that increase resiliency during Resource Account provisioning. This update helps device admins reduce setup failures and benefit from general Authenticator reliability enhancements.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-device-apps

- **Teams Rooms on Android certified devices**

  Added new certified firmware entries for multiple Cisco devices, including the latest Teams client and management app versions, released July 29, 2026, for DoD and GCCH clouds. This expands certified coverage and ensures organizations can deploy current builds across regulated environments.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-hardware-android

- **Certified Teams panels**

  Added Cisco Navigator to the certified Teams panels list with current firmware and Microsoft app versions, available for GCCH. This broadens certified hardware options and supports standardized deployments in government cloud environments.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/teams-panels-certified-hardware

- **Troubleshooting the new optimization**

  Expanded the error code mapping with root causes and remediation steps, including new entry 12002 (internet timeout), enhanced guidance for 12030 (connection aborted), and clarifications for 1460, 1722, 15616, 3002, and 16389. These updates improve diagnostics with actionable steps (such as using NAT Tool and checking Event Viewer), helping admins resolve VDI optimization issues more efficiently.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/vdi-2-troubleshooting
