# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-08-01 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Security  

## Major Changes

- **User reported security submissions report in Microsoft Teams**

  Expanded the article from a calling-only view to a comprehensive guide covering calling, chats and channels, and user-reported submissions. Added prerequisites for required Teams admin policy settings, updated steps for filtering by category and date, and refreshed visuals. Introduced detailed field definitions (such as Submission ID, Call ID, MRIs), participant and tenant/domain attributes, CSV-only fields, and export guidance to improve investigation and reporting workflows.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/end-user-reported-security-signal

- **Meetings and events feature and capacity comparison**

  Updated Registration attendee capacity from up to 20,000 to up to 1,000. This change affects event planning and registration strategies, requiring organizers to adjust capacity expectations and consider alternatives for larger audiences. Review existing registrations and communications to ensure alignment with the new limits.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/meetings-events-feature-comparison

- **Troubleshooting the new optimization**

  Expanded troubleshooting guidance for error 16389 (E_FAIL) to clarify causes and resolution steps. Documented Intune MDM applicability, the BlockNonAdminUserInstall CSP policy and its registry path, and how E_ACCESSDENIED surfaces as windows_error_16389 with fallback behavior. Added a likelihood matrix covering AppLocker/WDAC, SlimCore Host/Framework registration mismatches and shared kiosk issues (with fixes noted in Plugin 2026.29.1.4), and temp folder access problems to speed root-cause analysis.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/vdi-2-troubleshooting

## Moderate Changes

- **Release Notes for Teams Android Devices Management Apps**

  Added a July 2026 release entry and updated the supported versions table. Expanded Android device management with provisioning in Pro Management Portal and new device settings support (for example, Hotline and line keys), along with stability fixes. These updates streamline deployment and configuration for Teams Android devices.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-device-apps
