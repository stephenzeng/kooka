# Microsoft Teams
**Date created:** 2026-07-16 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Agent, Analytics, Monitoring, Security  

## New Articles

- **User reported security signals report**

  Introduced a new report in the Teams admin center that surfaces user-submitted security signals for Calls under Analytics & reports > Protection reports. The article explains how to access and run the report, including selecting interaction mode and date range. It notes that reporting a call must be enabled as a prerequisite. A comprehensive field reference outlines columns and export-only fields such as submission ID, call duration, participants, and tenant/user identifiers, helping admins triage and investigate reports effectively.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/end-user-reported-security-signal

## Moderate Changes

- **Reference - Supported Configurations**

  Added a new limit for Call representative alert time with a minimum of 15 seconds and a maximum of 180 seconds. Clarified that on Teams for Windows with a Bluetooth audio device using call control buttons, the maximum ring time is capped at 120 seconds.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-reference-limits-supported-configurations

- **Reporting - Teams Phone Agent, Auto Attendant, and Call Queue historical reports**

  Updated template references to v0.03 and expanded supported locales beyond English (United States) to include English (Australia) and English (United Kingdom). Guidance has been adjusted to reflect the new template version and language options.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-report-historical

- **Setup - Teams Phone Agent**

  Updated Known issues to reflect Historical reporting v0.03 and expanded supported locales to include English (Australia) and English (United Kingdom). Removed outdated notes about untested/unauthorized users and refreshed What’s new to July 14 announcing the v0.03 reporting update.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-setup-teams-phone-agent

- **Manage devices in the Teams admin center with administrative units**

  Added an IMPORTANT notice that device monitoring, management, and updates are moving to the Pro Management Portal, with a link to the transition guide. Admins should begin using the Pro Management Portal to ensure continuity.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/administrative-unit

- **Release Notes for Teams Android Devices Management Apps**

  Added a July 2026 release (1.0.0.202607020009) for the Teams admin agent app. The release introduces Android device management and provisioning in the Pro Management Portal, adds new settings (Hotline, line keys), and includes bug fixes.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-device-apps

- **Teams Rooms certified systems and peripherals**

  Reclassified several devices from Certified to the End of Service/Life section, including HP Slice Partner Ready with Logitech TAP, Lenovo ThinkSmart One variants, Logitech Tap, and Lenovo ThinkSmart Tiny. These devices are no longer supported as certified hardware.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/certified-hardware

- **Teams Rooms on Android certified devices**

  Added a new vendor section for Shure with IntelliMix Bar Pro IMXBP and IntelliMix Touch Panel IMXTP10, including firmware, apps, and release dates. Updated Poly Studio G62 status to “Recalled” in all applicable device tables.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/devices/certified-hardware-android

- **Microsoft Teams device health monitoring**

  Added an IMPORTANT notice that device monitoring, management, and updates are moving to the Pro Management Portal, with a transition guide link. Admins are encouraged to shift workflows to the new portal.

- **Microsoft Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal device update management**

  Revised guidance to position the Pro Management Portal as the primary solution for device update management, now supporting all Teams Android device types and signaling upcoming deprecation of Teams Admin Center for Android device management. Clarified that firmware and application updates for Android devices are supported in PMP. Added an important notice strongly recommending PMP for validated updates and warning of risks and lack of guaranteed updates with alternative methods.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/update-management
