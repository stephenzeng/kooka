# Microsoft Viva
**Date created:** 2026-08-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Configuration, Guidance, Monitoring  

## Moderate Changes

- **Advanced analysis metrics**

  Updated metrics guidance to explain an upcoming methodology change starting in late April/early May 2026: some feature-level and app-level user/action metrics for Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint may decline as conversational and edit-mode prompts are tracked separately. Clarified that during the interim, total active Copilot and app-level user/action counts still include edit-mode prompts and are not affected. Readers should interpret short-term trends with care as tracking transitions to the refined approach.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/insights/advanced/reference/metrics

- **Upload and maintain data through the Microsoft 365 admin center**

  Rebranded the experience to “Microsoft 365 Organizational Data Service,” updating headings, FAQs, procedures, and the attribute mapping table for consistency. Clarified EffectiveDate handling for single and multiple-date scenarios and refreshed upload tips and troubleshooting, including guidance for large file uploads via SharePoint.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/insights/advanced/admin/upload-org-data-admin-center
