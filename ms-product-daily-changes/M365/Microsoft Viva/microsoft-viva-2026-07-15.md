# Microsoft Viva
**Date created:** 2026-07-15 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure conversation settings for the Viva Engage network**

  Updated guidance on conversation GIF settings by switching the third‑party provider from Tenor to GIPHY. Clarified that Engage uses GIPHY’s most restrictive content filtering, outlined reporting through organizational support processes, and noted that enabling GIFs is subject to GIPHY’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy—helping admins manage content controls and compliance expectations.
