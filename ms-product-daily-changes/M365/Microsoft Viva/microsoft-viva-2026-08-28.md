# Microsoft Viva
**Date created:** 2026-08-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Guidance, Monitoring  

## Major Changes

- **Connect to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard for Microsoft 365 customers**

  Introduced a new Usage levels insight that classifies users as Power, Habitual, Novice, or Other based on frequency and consistency of Copilot actions over a rolling 12-week period. Added visuals and drill-downs including Usage levels card, trends over time, breakdowns by group, adoption by level and app, and drivers of power usage with configurable thresholds and view options. This update improves visibility into adoption quality, enabling teams to target enablement and measure progress more effectively. Subsequent insights were renumbered and screenshots updated to align with the new structure.
