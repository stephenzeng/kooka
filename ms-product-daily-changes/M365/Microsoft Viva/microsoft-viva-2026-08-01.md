## New Articles

- **Identification in Copilot Analytics**

  Introduced a preview article explaining how Copilot Analytics can surface identified outputs (instead of only anonymized metrics) to enable actions like finding champions, targeting training, and tracking rollout impact. Governance is managed through a Viva Feature Access Management policy that Microsoft 365 Global or AI Administrators can configure. The article details setup via the Microsoft 365 admin center or PowerShell (Add-VivaModuleFeaturePolicy) with consistent policy visibility across both methods. It also outlines scoping options at group/user level (with limits) or tenant-wide and links to feature access management guidance.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/insights/identification-copilot-analytics

## Moderate Changes

- **Export data from the Agent Dashboard (preview)**

  Updated the article to reflect preview status and added a prominent note clarifying that identifiable export is limited to public preview customers and may change before general availability. The update also highlights personal data processing implications and references the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum.
