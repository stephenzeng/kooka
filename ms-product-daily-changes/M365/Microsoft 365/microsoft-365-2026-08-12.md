## New Articles

- **Compliance Reporting for Microsoft 365 Backup**

  Introduced new documentation for compliance reports that show real-time backup enrollment status across SharePoint, OneDrive, and Exchange. The article explains status categories, error visibility through detailed views, and how to filter and search by policy and item enrollment to quickly find issues. It includes step-by-step guidance to access and navigate reports in the admin center, plus a consolidated pane for failed items and policy-level breakdowns. It also previews upcoming metrics for restore point availability and storage consumption, with interim cost tracking via Azure Cost Management.
