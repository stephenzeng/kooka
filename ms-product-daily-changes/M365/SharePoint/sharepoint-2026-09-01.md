# SharePoint
**Date created:** 2026-09-01 UTC  
**Tags:** Guidance, Troubleshooting  

## New Articles

- **Restore deleted items from the site collection recycle bin**

  Introduced a how-to article on restoring deleted items from the site collection (second-stage) Recycle Bin in SharePoint Online. Clarifies differences between first- and second-stage Recycle Bins, storage/quota impact, and how web items behave, reducing confusion with the Windows Recycle Bin. Provides step-by-step navigation for modern team, classic, and communication sites to reach the second-stage bin and restore items, including cases where the original folder was deleted. Details retention policies (93 days) and the additional 14-day backup restore via Microsoft Support, and highlights Microsoft 365 Backup for extended protection and faster recovery. Explains restoration behavior for securable objects and dependencies, helping admins anticipate outcomes during recovery.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/restore-deleted-items-from-site-collection-recycle-bin

## Moderate Changes

- **SharePoint data deletion in Microsoft 365**

  Expanded deletion guidance with step-by-step procedures for removing single or multiple items and emptying the site Recycle Bin, with screenshots for clarity. Added instructions for accessing and managing the second-stage (site collection) Recycle Bin across modern and classic sites, plus cautions about permanent deletion and what to do when the Recycle Bin link is not visible. This improves usability and reduces errors when permanently deleting content.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/sharepoint-data-deletion
