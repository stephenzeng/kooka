## Moderate Changes

- **Data access governance reports - get site permission states snapshot report for SharePoint sites**
  
  Updated guidance clarifies report coverage: sites in NoAccess lock status and archived sites are excluded, while unlocked and ReadOnly sites are included. This helps administrators interpret gaps in report results and set accurate expectations when auditing permissions.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/data-access-governance-site-permissions-report
