## New Articles

- **Standard storage for unlicensed OneDrive accounts**

  Introduced a new article that explains how to opt in to Standard storage for unlicensed OneDrive accounts and enable pay-as-you-go billing in the Microsoft 365 admin center. Covers tenant-wide active-storage period controls (from 93 up to 3,650 days) and account-level extensions using PowerShell, along with lifecycle milestones such as read-only and archive states. Provides step-by-step admin workflows to set, verify, extend, and revert storage states, plus best practices, pricing considerations, and troubleshooting. Notes general availability timing and clarifies where to configure billing versus when to use PowerShell.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/standard-storage-unlicensed-onedrive-accounts

## Moderate Changes

- **Manage unlicensed OneDrive user accounts**

  Updated guidance to clarify that lifecycle timelines reflect default tenant settings and that admins can adjust the tenant-wide active-storage period for unlicensed accounts. Clarifies that reactivated accounts are active for 30 days by default and explains how to extend activity beyond 30 days using standard storage settings. Expands archiving details, including the default 93-day period and how to modify it, and adds a related reference to Standard storage.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/unlicensed-onedrive-accounts
