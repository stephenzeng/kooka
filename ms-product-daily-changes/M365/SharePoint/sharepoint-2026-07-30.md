## Moderate Changes

- **Change your SharePoint domain name**

  Updated policy allows organizations to rename their SharePoint domain up to two times per tenant, with a mandatory 30-day wait before scheduling another rename after one completes. This change provides greater flexibility for rebranding and tenant consolidation while setting clear guardrails to reduce operational risk.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/change-your-sharepoint-domain-name
