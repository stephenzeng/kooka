# SharePoint
**Date created:** 2026-07-29 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, AI, Governance, Security  

## Major Changes

- **Restrict discovery of SharePoint sites and content**

  Expanded Restricted Content Discovery to limit organization-wide search and Microsoft 365 Copilot from surfacing content from designated SharePoint sites, including files users recently interacted with. Removed AI entry points on affected sites, such as the Copilot button, AI actions (like creating agents), and “Create pages with AI,” to reduce unintended exposure paths. Updated guidance and cautions to consistently reference organization-wide search and Copilot experiences. Clarified that prior allowances for discovering owned or recently accessed content no longer apply under these restrictions.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/restricted-content-discovery
