**Date created:** 2026-07-24 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Manage unlicensed OneDrive user accounts**
  
  Updated lifecycle guidance to specify that unlicensed OneDrive accounts are archived on day 93, removing the previous possibility of moving them to the recycle bin. This clarifies retention expectations and helps admins align automation and governance policies with the new archival behavior.
  
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/unlicensed-onedrive-accounts
