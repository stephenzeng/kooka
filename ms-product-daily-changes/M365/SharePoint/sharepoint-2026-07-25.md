# SharePoint
**Date created:** 2026-07-25 UTC  
**Tags:** AI, Administration  

## Major Changes

- **How to Enable Enterprise Brand Images with PowerPoint Copilot**

  Reworked guidance prioritizes Microsoft 365 Brand Kit as the central source for on-brand images in PowerPoint Copilot. Added steps to publish Brand Kits and clarified the end-to-end user workflow for selecting a kit inside Copilot. Updated guidance explains how to connect OAL and Templafy libraries to Brand Kit so assets appear in Copilot via the kit. Streamlined recommendations focus on ensuring sufficient asset coverage and strong metadata quality. Deprecated the standalone OAL connector section while retaining key Templafy connector requirements as a note.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/enable-enterprise-brand-images-powerpoint-copilot

- **Manage unlicensed OneDrive user accounts**

  Introduces a cumulative 365-day nonpayment policy effective July 1, 2026, with key milestones: read-only at day 60, archive at day 93, eDiscovery availability at day 275, and deletion risk at day 365; EDU, GCC, and DoD are excluded. Clarifies behavior for accounts linked to deleted versus active identities and outlines how licensing, billing status, and retention mechanisms interact. Strengthens Microsoft Purview/eDiscovery guidance by confirming archived content remains discoverable/exportable without reactivation and warning that deletion renders held content unrecoverable. Expands administrative guidance with timelines, examples, and actions to maintain compliance and manage billing. A significantly expanded FAQ covers reactivation and relicensing steps, external sharing impacts, reporting field behavior, and enforcement details regardless of Microsoft 365 Archive enablement.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/unlicensed-onedrive-accounts
