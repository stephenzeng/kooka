- **Integration for LinkedIn overview**

  Introduced documentation for enabling and configuring the LinkedIn integration in the Recruiting add-on, including prerequisites (LinkedIn Recruiter/job posting license, admin role, company page permissions). Provides guided setup steps for accepting terms, selecting the company and careers site URL, authenticating via the LinkedIn premium job widget, and configuring contract and default job poster. Explains the end-to-end job posting workflow to LinkedIn, how to monitor status and retrieve the posting URL, and what candidates experience when redirected to the Careers portal. Clarifies LinkedIn contract and Contract ID requirements for premium jobs and includes troubleshooting and FAQ guidance for delays, reactivations, failures, and common configuration issues, with emphasis on secure, controlled API communication.
