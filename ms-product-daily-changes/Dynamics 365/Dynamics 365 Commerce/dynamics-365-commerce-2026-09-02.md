## New Articles

- **Manage database storage in Dynamics 365 Commerce**

  Introduces a central guide to reduce and govern database storage across Commerce headquarters and CSU channel databases. Provides prescriptive options for payment token lifecycle (archival to Azure Blob Storage, permanent deletion, and in-place compression) with prerequisites and impacted fields, so teams can safely lower costs and risk. Explains how to retain or purge posted Commerce transactions using Dataverse long-term retention, POS/online cart and transaction retention settings, and the DB maintenance agent (public preview) for CSU enforcement. Details when to use the Supply Chain Management archive framework for fully invoiced sales orders and how to monitor table-level growth via the Power Platform admin center. Highlights safeguards, roles, scopes, and date restrictions to keep changes compliant with legal and business requirements.
