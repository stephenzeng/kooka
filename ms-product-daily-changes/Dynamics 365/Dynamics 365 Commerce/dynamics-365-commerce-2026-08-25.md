## New Articles

- **Preview features in Dynamics 365 Commerce 10.0.49 (July 2026)**

  Introduced a new preview What's New article covering the 10.0.49 release schedule and key capabilities across POS, e-commerce, payments, and B2B. POS improvements include a modern customer details page, a web-based receipt designer, faster cart and line operations, keyboard shortcuts, and iOS contextual switching—boosting cashier productivity and configurability. Operations and order capture are enhanced with expedited warehouse release, better notes, pickup defaults, and deposit overrides, while payments add receipt printing on supported Adyen terminals and improved token handling. Platform updates modernize the tech stack (Node.js 22/24, TypeScript 4.2.4) and enable OBO scenarios with Microsoft Entra External ID; B2B gains multi-outlet management with roles/permissions, stronger user management, invoice access and delivery, and improved OBO flows. The article also links to platform updates, bug fixes, the release wave plan, and removed/deprecated features.
