## Moderate Changes

- **Dynamics 365 Payment Connector for Adyen overview**

  Updated the Important notice to reflect Adyen’s PCI 6 migration timeline. Starting April 30, 2027, PCI 5 terminals (such as those on firmware V1_120) will receive only limited, high-severity bug fixes, signaling reduced support for PCI 5-certified devices. Merchants should plan to upgrade to PCI 6 hardware to maintain full support and compliance.
