## New Articles

- **Payment Amount Is 0.00 in Online Store Transactions**

  Introduced a troubleshooting article explaining why the Payment amount shows 0.00 for credit card orders in online store transactions until fulfillment. Clarifies the authorize-versus-capture flow and that the Payment amount reflects only captured funds, so orders remain in Authorized status until invoiced or shipped. Provides guidance on where to view payment status and confirms no action is required if funds are only authorized. Includes pointers to related authorization and capture documentation for deeper understanding.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/commerce/payments/payment-amount-zero-online-store-transactions
