## Major Changes

- **Certified partner solutions for Microsoft for Manufacturing**

  Reorganized solution categories and refreshed listings to reflect current market offerings and AI-driven operations. Added multiple partner solutions (for example, Aras Innovator SaaS, HCLTech PLM Solution, Avanade External Production, EY Inventory Optimization, Litmus Edge Manager, Capgemini EaaS, Wipro Asset Management, Kongsberg Kognitwin, Adobe Real-Time CDP), updated several entries, and removed deprecated offerings. This overhaul streamlines discovery and selection of validated solutions across product lifecycle, supply chain, operations, and customer experiences.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/manufacturing/appendix/certified-partner-solutions-manufacturing

- **Intelligent factories**

  Introduced a new Maintenance and quality section covering predictive maintenance, AI-powered inspection, and root-cause analysis with supporting Microsoft technologies. Added a reference architecture for Industrial Edge with Azure AI and updated edge and infrastructure partner solutions to highlight factory copilots and new edge managers. Refined worker enablement technologies and expanded AI/analytics governance by incorporating Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Purview, and Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, helping manufacturers operationalize AI at scale.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/manufacturing/enable-intelligent-factories

- **Key Microsoft technologies supporting Microsoft for Manufacturing**

  Expanded coverage with new sections for Dynamics 365 Finance, Microsoft Purview, and Microsoft Sustainability Manager, strengthening finance, governance, and sustainability guidance. Rebranded Azure AI Foundry to Microsoft Foundry and updated references (for example, Microsoft Sales Copilot, Microsoft 365 for frontline workers) to align with current product naming. Refined multiple product descriptions to emphasize concrete manufacturing use cases and operational outcomes.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/manufacturing/appendix/microsoft-technology

- **Digital engineering**

  Restructured content to focus on Product lifecycle and design, Digital twins and simulations, and Connected products. Added new technologies and partner solutions, plus reference architectures for key PLM/CAD scenarios (for example, Siemens Teamcenter on Oracle Database@Azure, Teamcenter with Nasuni, Siemens NX/X on Azure Virtual Desktop) and Ansys Discovery on Azure. Replaced legacy terminology with Microsoft Foundry and clarified how these capabilities accelerate design, simulation, and connected product innovation.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/manufacturing/unlock-innovation
