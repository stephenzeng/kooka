# Dynamics 365 Industry Solutions
**Date created:** 2026-08-12 UTC  
**Tags:** Compliance, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure emission factors**

  Updated the emission factor libraries table by adding 2026 to the Defra library and standardizing year ordering across EPA (descending with combined 2020–2021), IEA (consolidated 2015–2022 with 2025 listed), NGER (2025 then 2023), and EXIOBASE (2022–2019). These adjustments improve accuracy and consistency when selecting factors and reduce confusion about available years.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/sustainability/calculate-emission-factors

- **Overview of Microsoft for Manufacturing reference architectures**

  Reorganized the overview into Digital engineering, Resilient supply chains, and Intelligent factories, and regrouped scenario links accordingly (for example, Siemens Industrial Edge under Intelligent factories and Icertis Contract Intelligence under Resilient supply chains). Updated the important note to reflect inclusion of Microsoft technologies, partner solutions, and hardware, making it easier to navigate scenarios by theme and understand solution scope.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/manufacturing/architecture/ra-overview
