# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-09-01 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure Copilot features in the help pane**

  Updated terminology and labels throughout the article and clarified end-to-end configuration steps, especially for knowledge filters and custom instructions. Standardized section structure and headings, refined descriptions for enhanced features and proactive insights, and clarified the Bing Search requirement for trusted webpages. Includes a renamed Admin Management agent (preview) section; no functional changes, but clearer and more consistent guidance.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/copilot-enable-help-pane

- **Customize Copilot conversation summaries**

  Rewrote and reorganized the steps to configure summary formats, with clearer choices between Paragraph and Structured layouts. Added guidance for adding and reordering custom sections, resetting to defaults, using standardized section names, removing missing information from outputs, and saving changes. Helps admins create consistent, concise summaries that fit their workflow.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/customize-copilot-conv-summary

- **Summarize conversations with Copilot in non-Microsoft CRM systems**

  Replaced the navigation guidance with a clear, step-by-step procedure to open conversation summarization in embedded experiences. Users can now follow explicit steps to sign in, open a conversation, and launch summarization from the communication panel.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/embed-summarize-conversation

- **Draft emails with Copilot**

  Retitled and streamlined the flow for drafting, refining, and sending emails with clearer steps to open the experience from the Copilot help pane. Clarified how to use Copilot-recommended templates and standardized terminology (for example, “Use knowledge sources”). Refined instructions for partial drafts and reorganized related sections for faster, more accurate email authoring.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/use-copilot-email
