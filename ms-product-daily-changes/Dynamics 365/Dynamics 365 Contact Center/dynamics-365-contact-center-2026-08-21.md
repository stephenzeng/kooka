- **View and analyze forecast results**

  Introduced step-by-step guidance for opening and analyzing Workforce Management forecast results, including using scenario reports. Added instructions for applying filters by date range, channels, and queues; toggling AHT; and switching between intraday and hierarchical time aggregations. Clarified how to interpret the volume chart and data table, including differences between aggregate and per-dimension views and when actuals become available. Included procedures to export table data to Excel and manage forecast snapshots (select, rename, and revert) to streamline analysis and decision-making.
