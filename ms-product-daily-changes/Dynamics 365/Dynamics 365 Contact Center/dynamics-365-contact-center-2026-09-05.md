## New Articles

- **Set up consult with AI agents during voice conversations (preview)**

  Introduces a step-by-step guide to enable consultation with voice-enabled AI agents during voice calls (preview). Covers prerequisites and configuration in Copilot Service admin center, including selecting AI agents and setting transcription/recording and hold behavior. Provides instructions to configure the AI agent in Copilot Studio using topics to play messages, capture consent, and transfer the conversation back to the representative. Explains consultation call flow and timeout/failure handling so admins can set expectations and design reliable experiences.
