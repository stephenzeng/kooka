# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-07-19 UTC  
**Tags:** Agent  

## Major Changes

- **Transparency note for real-time agents in Dynamics 365 Contact Center**

  Expanded guidance from voice-only to multi-channel real-time agents, covering both telephony and digital messaging/chat to reflect broader deployment scenarios. Updated definitions, key terms, and architecture options, including the addition of GPT Realtime Mini, and clarified system behavior across voice and chat flows. Enhanced considerations on latency, accessibility, multilingual support, information disclosure, and one-time configuration constraints, with revised performance metrics and error types applicable to customers across channels. Refreshed evaluation and best practices to emphasize automated and human assessments, scenario coverage, and Responsible AI for multi-channel interactions.
