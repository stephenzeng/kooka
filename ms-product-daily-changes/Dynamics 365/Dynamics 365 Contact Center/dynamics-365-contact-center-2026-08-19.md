# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-08-19 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Configuration, Get Started, Guidance  

## New Articles

- **Overview of forecasting**

  Introduced a high-level guide to Workforce Management forecasting that explains what’s estimated (volume and average handle time) across channels and queues and how results support capacity planning and scheduling. Outlines short-term, long-term, and preview weighted-average scenarios with their horizons and intervals, helping teams choose the right approach. Highlights data quality practices to improve accuracy, notes required privileges and regional availability, and links to related setup and how-to topics.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/workforce-management-forecast-overview

- **Forecast with weighted average (preview)**

  Added step-by-step guidance for creating weighted-average forecast scenarios using externally uploaded historical data. Describes selecting the forecast entity, targeting specific channels/queues/dates, and weighting source days with Manual, Recency, or Equal modes to fine-tune results. Shows how to review outputs such as intraday distributions and confidence intervals, and clarifies preview limits, including external-data-only support.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/workforce-management-forecast-weighted-average

## Major Changes

- **Create and manage forecast scenarios**

  Substantially revised the article to clarify scenario concepts and add responsible-use guidance, making it easier to select and configure short- and long-term forecasts. Expanded prerequisites and step-by-step setup, including configuration parameters, historical data options, and a new automated refresh schedule to keep forecasts current. Added comprehensive management guidance for editing scenarios, creating snapshots, reviewing outputs and job history, and handling deletion constraints. Removed legacy dashboard-centric steps and manual run instructions to streamline operations and focus on snapshot-driven workflows.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/workforce-management-forecast-scenarios
