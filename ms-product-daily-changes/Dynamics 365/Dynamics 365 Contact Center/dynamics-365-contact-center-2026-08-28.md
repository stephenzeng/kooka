## New Articles

- **Use the Quality Assurance Agent dashboard in Dynamics 365 Contact Center**

  Introduced a new article for the Quality Assurance Agent historical analytics dashboard to help supervisors monitor quality performance. It explains prerequisites, how to enable historical analytics, and how to work with default and customized filters using bookmarks. The article details KPI cards, charts for quality trends and threshold breaches, and a representative-level performance table to identify coaching needs. It also includes availability notes, responsible-use guidance, and related resources to accelerate adoption.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/quality-assurance-agent-dashboard
