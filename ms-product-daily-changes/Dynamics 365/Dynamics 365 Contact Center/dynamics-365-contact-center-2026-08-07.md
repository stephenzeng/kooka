# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-08-07 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Manage Dynamics 365 Contact Center environments**

  Updated guidance expands beyond reset/restore/recover to include migration scenarios and moving a contact center between geographies. It adds step-by-step post-move actions—removing administration mode, obtaining the new organization URL, re-embedding the chat widget, and updating Azure Communication Services webhook endpoints—using Copilot Service admin center and Azure portal. It also clarifies that record routing resumes after administration mode is removed and retains tenant-to-tenant migration instructions.
