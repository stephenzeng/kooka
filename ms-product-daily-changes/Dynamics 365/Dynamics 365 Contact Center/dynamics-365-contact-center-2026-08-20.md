- **Use workforce alerts for volume spikes (preview)**

  Introduced preview documentation for workforce volume spike alerts that helps supervisors react to unexpected demand in near real time. The article explains prerequisites and setup in Copilot Service admin center, then provides step-by-step guidance to create alerts with key parameters such as linked forecast, deviation thresholds, duration, and recipients. It details how alerts are evaluated on a five-minute cadence with consecutive-interval logic, and how to review and act on alert details. Best practices and preview limitations are included to optimize thresholds and expectations during early adoption.
