## New Articles

- **Configure bullseye routing using conversation orchestration playbooks (preview)**

  Introduced a step-by-step guide for setting up bullseye routing with conversation orchestration playbooks, including prerequisites and configuration steps. Explains progressive assignment through levels, how priority interacts with assignment strategies, and how presence, capacity, and skills checks are applied. Details configuration of cumulative wait times, simultaneous level expansions, and fallback rules, with a worked example to illustrate behavior. Provides guidance on extending scenarios with dynamic prioritization or overflow playbooks and includes instructions for creating and managing user groups for queues.
