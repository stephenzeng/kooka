## Moderate Changes

- **Monitor representatives in real time (preview)**

  Updated the Representatives view guidance to indicate that you can only reset a representative’s presence status. References to modifying skills and capacity profiles were removed to avoid implying unsupported actions. This clarification aligns expectations for admins using real-time monitoring.
