# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-07-15 UTC  
**Tags:** AI, Administration, Agent, Analytics  

## Major Changes

- **Manage Customer Intent Agent**

  Added support for emails in rule creation and clarified that each line of business can have only one rule for cases and one for conversations; repetition guidance now includes emails. Streamlined setup by removing the optional Apply to historical data step. Redesigned intent discovery with a required Data source rules configuration to filter eligible cases, conversations, and emails, improved simulation to generate and review intents and groups, replaced Export to Excel with View job details, and refined failure messaging. Introduced a Promote intents to intent library workflow to review discovery jobs, promote selected intents or groups, and mark jobs as reviewed.
