# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-08-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Session closure reasons**

  Expanded the catalog of session closure reasons to include new entries covering external agent actions, connectivity problems, capacity limits, timeouts, and auto-reject scenarios. New reasons include ManuallyClosedSession; ExternalAgentAccept/Reject/Transfer; VoiceConnectivityProblem; MaximumSessionsReached; CallTimedOut; Customer/Agent non-response timeout rules; AgentMovedConversationToWaiting; and auto-reject cases tied to browser refresh or outbound calls, each with clear descriptions. This improves accuracy in reporting, troubleshooting, and automation that relies on consistent closure reason taxonomy.
