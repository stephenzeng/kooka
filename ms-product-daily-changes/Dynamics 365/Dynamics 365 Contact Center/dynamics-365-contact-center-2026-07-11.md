# Dynamics 365 Contact Center
**Date created:** 2026-07-11 UTC  
**Tags:** AI, Administration, Agent, Governance  

## New Articles

- **Overview of Service Operations Agent in Dynamics 365 Contact Center**

  Introduced an overview of the AI-powered Service Operations Agent that centralizes conversational administration for setup, routing, and troubleshooting. Highlights include guided provisioning flows, natural language configuration of channels, queues, and routing rules, and comprehensive management of workstreams, operating hours, capacity profiles, skills, and users. The article also outlines voice/Teams extensibility and links to related orchestration and responsible AI guidance to accelerate deployment and governance.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/overview-service-operations-agent

- **Responsible AI FAQ for Service Operations Agent**

  Added a Responsible AI FAQ detailing the agent’s intended uses for administrators and implementation teams, evaluation approach, and performance metrics. It explains known limitations and mitigation strategies, and provides guidance on safe, verified operation through clear inputs, oversight, and validation. The FAQ also lists feedback channels and references related usage documentation to support informed adoption.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/implement/service-operations-agent-rai-faq

## Major Changes

- **Configure screen and audio recording with desktop companion application**

  Expanded guidance from screen-only to combined screen and audio recording, including new tenant-level controls in Copilot Service admin center. Added prerequisites for workstream-level recording and transcription, clarified that tenant-level audio settings override workstream configuration, and noted that screen and audio features operate independently. Introduced options to toggle the red border indicator and select multiple monitors, and documented default behaviors (audio on by default; screen off until enabled). Included steps for authorized roles to play back screen recordings within closed conversations in Customer Service workspace to support compliance and review.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/configure-screen-recording

- **Use Service Operations Agent in Dynamics 365 Contact Center**

  Removed preview status and documented broader capabilities across routing configuration and administration. Added a detailed configuration section covering workstreams, queues, route-to-queue rules (including rulesets, hit policy, reordering, and constraints), operating hours, capacity profiles, skills, and users, with clarified assignment methods and channel alignment. Enhanced examples for prompts (such as creating route-to-queue rules), refined Dataverse connection guidance via MCP, and updated related links to support end-to-end enablement.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/use-service-operations-agent
