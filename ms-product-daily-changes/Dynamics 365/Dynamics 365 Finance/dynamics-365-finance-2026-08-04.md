# Dynamics 365 Finance
**Date created:** 2026-08-04 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Settlement overview**

  Updated guidance on large-volume payment settlements with performance warnings when payment journal line settlement exceeds 2,000 open transactions or payment proposals exceed 5,000. Provides actions to prevent slowdowns and timeouts, including reducing marked transactions, rerunning proposals with tighter filters, removing unnecessary open transactions, or proceeding with processing as needed.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/cash-bank-management/settlement-overview
