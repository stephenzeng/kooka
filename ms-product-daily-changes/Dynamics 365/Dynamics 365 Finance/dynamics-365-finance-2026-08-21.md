# Dynamics 365 Finance
**Date created:** 2026-08-21 UTC  
**Tags:** Compliance  

## Moderate Changes

- **What's new or changed in Dynamics 365 Finance 10.0.49 (September 2026)**

  Reclassified four tax features in 10.0.49 from On by default to Mandatory, covering batch synchronization of tax hierarchy versions, inclusion of India TCS in invoice totals, a new VAT register date calculation choice, and inheritance of the Empty tax base for outgoing tax flag on vendor invoice journal lines when reverse charge applies. This change enforces compliance defaults and standardizes behavior across tax processes. Finance admins should review environments for alignment ahead of or after upgrading to 10.0.49.
