**Date created:** 2026-07-14 UTC

## New Articles

- **Create fiscal notes for return in Brazil tax reform**

  Introduced guidance to create fiscal documents for goods returns aligned with Brazil’s tax reform. Covers returns from taxpayer customers (customer issues Return NFe) and from non-taxpayer customers (seller issues an incoming Return NFe on the customer’s behalf). Clarifies key settings such as the Full refusal toggle, Generate incoming fiscal document, and how Purpose of return maps to finNFe (including Credit Note with CFOP 1.949/2.949). Provides step-by-step setup and processing, including CFOP and tax groups configuration, invoicing, and verification so finance teams can post compliant return documents with fewer errors.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/localizations/brazil/brazil-reform-return-refusal

- **Set up and create fiscal notes for unprocessed invoices in Brazil tax reform**

  Added end-to-end instructions to configure and issue fiscal notes for unprocessed invoices in Accounts receivable under Brazil’s tax reform. Details parameter setup, creating the unprocessed invoice from an approved AR fiscal note, configuring line tax groups and CFOP, linking the original invoice, and posting. Highlights scope limits (AR only), CFOP compatibility, and tax behavior to help ensure compliance and accurate tax calculation.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/localizations/brazil/brazil-reform-unprocessed
