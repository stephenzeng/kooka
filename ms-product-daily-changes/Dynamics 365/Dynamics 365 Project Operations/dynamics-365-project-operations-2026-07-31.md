# Dynamics 365 Project Operations
**Date created:** 2026-07-31 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Other  

## New Articles

- **What's new July 2026 - Project Operations Core**

  Introduced July 2026 updates that streamline time tracking and planning, including weekly and time‑phased booking views for Team Members. Added Time Entry controls to cap daily hours and choose between time zone–aware or independent entry behavior, improving compliance and data consistency. Resource Management now includes Bulk Resource Reconciliation (Preview) to resolve shortages and excesses across multiple time slices with a single Reconcile All action, and Approvals supports a configurable first day of the week. Billing and Pricing now lets you set an invoice‑level accounting date to align revenue recognition and posting with Finance, and quality fixes address issues across Project/Resource Management, Billing, and Sales.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/project-operations/pro/whats-new/whats-new-july-2026-lite

- **What's new July 2026 - Project Operations Integrated with ERP**

  Published July 2026 updates with component versions (Dataverse 4.169.3470.3, Finance 10.0.48) and guidance to validate/upgrade dual‑write maps and troubleshoot missing columns. Enhanced Team Member and Approvals experiences with time‑phased booking visibility and a configurable first day of the week, and added Time Entry controls for maximum daily hours and time zone behavior. Introduced Bulk Resource Reconciliation (Preview) to reconcile staffing gaps and overallocations across multiple time slices, plus Project Financials improvements to specify bookable resources on general journals and speed posting of project invoice proposals with multithreaded batch tasks. Billing and Pricing now supports an invoice‑level accounting date to drive consistent posting across systems, and quality fixes cover Dataverse scenarios; Finance fixes are referenced via Lifecycle Services.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/project-operations/whats-new/whats-new-july-2026-resource-based
