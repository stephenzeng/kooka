# Dynamics 365 Business Central
**Date created:** 2026-07-11 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Troubleshooting the Shopify and Business Central synchronization**
  
  Updated troubleshooting guidance for order synchronization issues between Shopify and Business Central. The new section explains that archived orders are not imported, only orders created or modified after the last sync are considered, and request-page filters can exclude orders; it also directs users to review the Shopify Orders to Import page for errors and outlines how to reimport affected orders.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/shopify/troubleshoot
