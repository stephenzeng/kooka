## New Articles

- **Use the Expense Agent Mobile App (preview)**

  Introduced a how-to guide for the Expense Agent mobile app (preview) on iOS and Android, including availability, prerequisites, and sign-in behavior aligned with the web app. Clarifies required permissions and provides step-by-step guidance to capture receipts, scan multi-page documents, import photos/files, and share from other apps, with automatic extraction, categorization, and itemization. Explains adding mileage with calculation parity to the web app, managing expenses across reports, and submitting reports with emphasis on reimbursable amounts and payment methods. Details offline behavior (queued uploads and auto-refresh), data handling and privacy (no permanent local storage, AI-assisted processing), and a troubleshooting section to resolve common issues.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/expense-management/expense-agent-mobile-app
