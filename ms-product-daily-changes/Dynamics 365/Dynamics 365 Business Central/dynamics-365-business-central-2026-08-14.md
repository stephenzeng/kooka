# Dynamics 365 Business Central
**Date created:** 2026-08-14 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Guidance  

## New Articles

- **Use different component supply methods in subcontracting**

  Introduced a new how-to that explains three subcontracting component supply methods—Transfer to Vendor, Consignment at Vendor, and Vendor-Supplied—and how each affects planning and execution. Covers configuring routing and production BOM settings, vendor subcontracting location, and backward flushing. Demonstrates Planning Worksheet outcomes, including demand at the subcontractor for consignment, transfer orders for internal supply, and exclusion of vendor-supplied items. Walks through creating the subcontracting order, generating and posting transfers, receiving output, and verifying automatic consumption at the subcontracting location so teams can choose the right approach and plan accurately.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/contoso-coffee/manufacturing/subcontracting-component-supply-methods

- **Set up subcontracting prices and allocate item charges**

  Added a new guide to configure subcontracting price lists with minimum amounts and validate automatic price resolution during purchasing. Details creating and refreshing a production order, receiving and invoicing the subcontracting order, and allocating additional transport costs via item charges to the posted receipt. Shows how to reconcile expected vs. actual costs in production statistics, helping ensure accurate costing, pricing transparency, and better cost control.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/contoso-coffee/manufacturing/subcontracting-pricing-item-charges

## Major Changes

- **Set up and process a subcontracting operation**

  Expanded the article into a comprehensive, step-by-step scenario using Contoso Coffee data to set up routing, link codes, and Transfer to Vendor component supply, and to enable WIP transfer with clear descriptions. Added detailed guidance to create and refresh a production order, set vendor location codes, and generate a subcontracting purchase order directly from the routing. Included instructions to post transfer orders with component and WIP details, handle partial receipts, return unused materials, and post the vendor invoice with cost visibility in production statistics. This overhaul replaces a brief worksheet flow with a complete walkthrough that improves accuracy, traceability, and operational clarity for subcontracting.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/contoso-coffee/manufacturing/set-up-process-subcontracting-operation

## Moderate Changes
