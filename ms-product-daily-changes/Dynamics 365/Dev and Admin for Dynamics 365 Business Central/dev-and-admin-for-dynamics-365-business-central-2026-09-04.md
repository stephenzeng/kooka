## Moderate Changes

- **Extend the Shopify Connector**

  Expanded guidance on extending stock calculation for the Shopify Connector. Clarifies required mappings between the Shpfy Stock Calculation enum and export-eligibility interfaces, and explains that when mappings are missing or return false, inventory is calculated and stored but not exported to Shopify. Helps developers ensure correct exports and avoid unexpected sync gaps.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-extending-shopify
