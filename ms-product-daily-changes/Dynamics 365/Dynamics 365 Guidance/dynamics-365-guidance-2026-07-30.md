# Dynamics 365 Guidance
**Date created:** 2026-07-30 UTC  
**Tags:** Governance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Customer eligibility and partner qualifications**

  Expanded and itemized the list of Dynamics 365 applications eligible for FastTrack, naming Commerce, Customer Insights (Data and Journeys), Customer Service, Field Service, Finance, Human Resources, Project Operations, Sales, Supply Chain Management, and Microsoft Copilot for Sales/Service. This clarification reduces ambiguity about program scope and helps customers and partners quickly confirm eligibility.
