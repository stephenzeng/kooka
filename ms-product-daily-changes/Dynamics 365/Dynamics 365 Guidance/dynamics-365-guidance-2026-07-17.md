# Dynamics 365 Guidance
**Date created:** 2026-07-17 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics  

## Major Changes

- **Sample queries and dashboards for conversation diagnostics**

  Expanded troubleshooting guidance and end-to-end diagnostic queries for Dynamics 365 Contact Center using Application Insights and Kusto. Added a comprehensive Bullseye routing analysis that correlates lifecycle events, ring expansion, assignment outcomes, fallbacks, transfers, consults, and overflow with clear ID-to-name resolution and eligible user group tracking. Introduced targeted queries for fallback queues, overflow handling, rejected or delayed assignments, unassigned conversation reasons, and comparative analysis between agents, along with quick checks for presence, capacity, queue membership, capacity profiles, and skills. Improved clarity and consistency of instructions, and added references to Azure Data Explorer dashboards for visualization and continuous monitoring.
