# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-09-02 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Deprecation, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure payment profiles**

  Added a firm removal date (September 30, 2026) for the Apple Messages for Business channel in the Copilot Service admin center and updated the deprecations reference to aid planning. Prerequisite guidance now points to Apple Business Register, and step labels were clarified without changing core procedures. These updates help administrators plan channel continuity and follow the correct setup references.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-payment-profiles

- **Enable real-time customer sentiment with sentiment analysis**

  Updated availability guidance by removing a prior limitation note for sentiment notifications in Omnichannel for Customer Service on Unified Service Desk and streamlined setup instructions, including agent/supervisor thresholds. Corrected the alert level options to include “Very negative” and improved image captions, ensuring accurate configuration and clearer guidance.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/enable-sentiment-analysis
