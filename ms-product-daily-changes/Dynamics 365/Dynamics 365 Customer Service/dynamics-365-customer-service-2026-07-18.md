# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-07-18 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Agent  

## Moderate Changes

- **Overview of channels**

  Added a deprecation note for the Apple Messages for Business channel and linked to the official deprecations page. This clarifies channel availability so admins can plan migrations or alternative channel strategies.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/channels

- **Integrate a Copilot agent**

  Expanded guidance to emphasize building and governing agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio and reusing them across channels under consistent security and governance. Detailed real-time voice agent capabilities were outlined, including low-latency responses, interruption handling with context retention, multilingual switching, DTMF fallback, and proactive outreach. A new prerequisite clarifies that consumption-based Copilot credits are required for usage across voice and digital channels, with references to licensing resources.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-bot-virtual-agent

- **Configure payment profiles**

  Added a prominent note indicating that the Apple Messages for Business channel is deprecated, with a link to the deprecations page. This helps administrators avoid configuring deprecated channels and plan transitions to supported options.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-payment-profiles

- **Configure authentication settings**

  Inserted a deprecation notice in the Apple Messages for Business authentication section, linking to the deprecations page. This prevents new setups on a deprecated channel and guides readers to supported alternatives.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/create-chat-auth-settings

- **Create rich messages**

  Added a deprecation note for the Apple Messages for Business channel with a reference to the deprecations page. This ensures authors and admins focus rich messaging efforts on supported channels.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/create-rich-messages
