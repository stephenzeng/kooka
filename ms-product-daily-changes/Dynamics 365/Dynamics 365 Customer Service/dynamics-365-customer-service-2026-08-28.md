# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Configuration, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Manage omnichannel historical analytics reports in Customer Service**

  Added the Quality Assurance Agent dashboard to historical analytics, with availability limited to Dynamics 365 Contact Center, and added instructions to enable its historical analytics. Updated the setup for Copilot agents by removing the “Add advanced historical analytics for bot” step and directing users to define variables directly in Custom variables. Minor text refinements improve clarity without changing functionality.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/oc-historical-analytics-reports

- **Omnichannel for Customer Service dashboards**

  Expanded the dashboards list to include the Quality Assurance Agent historical analytics entry, noting it’s available only in Dynamics 365 Contact Center. Added guidance on enabling historical analytics for this dashboard to help administrators activate and use it effectively.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/omnichannel-analytics-insights
