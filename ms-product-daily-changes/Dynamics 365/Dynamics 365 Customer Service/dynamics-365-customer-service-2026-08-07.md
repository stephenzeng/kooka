# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-07 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Security  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure a Facebook channel**
  Added prerequisite guidance to plan for Meta App Review, including that the pages_messaging permission must be approved before customers outside app roles can message the page and that review times may vary. Clarified roles and permissions: a live app using pages_messaging requires App Review submission; only app‑role users can message before approval; and business verification may be required. These updates help administrators plan lead times and ensure compliance to avoid disruptions in customer messaging.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-facebook-channel
