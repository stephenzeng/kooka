## Moderate Changes

- **Resolve, Cancel, and Reassign Cases in Customer Service Hub**

  Updated guidance for reassigning cases when unified routing is enabled, instructing users to select Save & Route to initiate automatic routing. Clarifies that using Assign does not trigger auto-assignment, helping teams ensure work items enter the correct routing flow.
