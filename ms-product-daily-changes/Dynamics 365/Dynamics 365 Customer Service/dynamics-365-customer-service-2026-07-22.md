# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-07-22 UTC  
**Tags:** AI, Agent  

## Moderate Changes

- **Introduction to Dynamics 365 Contact Center**

  Updated guidance highlights in-session consults that bring experts into ongoing conversations without handoffs, preserving full context and accelerating resolution. The Voice section now covers consulting with internal users, specialist queues, or external PSTN numbers while keeping the customer context intact. The Microsoft Teams guidance adds starting consults directly from active conversations, using role/policy-based filtered consult lists, and leveraging skills- and availability-based routing for specialist queues.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/implement/introduction-omnichannel

- **Sentiment monitoring**

  Clarified how sentiment works by channel: real-time chat and messaging use a seven-level intensity based on the last six customer messages, while email uses a three-class model per message. Introduced case-level sentiment rollup across all related emails to give supervisors an overall indicator, with references to configuration steps for case sentiment analysis.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/supervisor-sentiment-monitoring

- **Transfer calls and consult with users in the voice channel**

  Added role- and rule-based filtered consults with a new prerequisite and section explaining how results are restricted across Representatives, Queue, and Teams tabs. Expanded consult-to-queue guidance to use skills- and availability-based routing, clarify that customers remain on hold during consults, and confirm conversation ownership only changes on explicit transfer.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/voice-channel-transfer-consult
