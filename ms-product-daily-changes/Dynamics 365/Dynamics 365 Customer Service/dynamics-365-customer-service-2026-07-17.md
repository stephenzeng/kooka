# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-07-17 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, AI, Automation  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure email classification in Customer Service**

  Updated guidance to add the email classification experience to the Email form in Power Apps, including adding the Email category (AI) field and the EmailClassificationControl component, selecting client targets (Web, Mobile, Tablet), and publishing. This enables agents to view AI-generated categories, adjust them, and provide feedback directly in the form; wording and label refinements were also made without changing functionality.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-email-classification
