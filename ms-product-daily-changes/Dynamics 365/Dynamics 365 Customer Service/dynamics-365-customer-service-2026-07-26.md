# Dynamics 365 Customer Service
**Date created:** 2026-07-26 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Agent, Governance  

## Major Changes

- **Configure an Apple Messages for Business channel**

  Announced deprecation of the Apple Messages for Business channel effective July 17, 2026. The article is scheduled for removal after September 30, 2026, signaling that the capability is being retired from product guidance. Customers should plan migrations to supported alternatives and review deprecation guidance to avoid service disruption.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/configure-apple-messages-for-business-channel

- **Deprecations in Customer Service**

  Added two deprecations: Apple Messages for Business channel (effective July 17, 2026) with removal from the Copilot Service admin center on September 30, 2026, and Customer support swarming (preview) deprecated effective February 8, 2026. Organizations should transition from Apple Messages for Business to supported channels and adopt embedded Microsoft Teams chat for collaboration in place of swarming. This guidance helps teams prepare roadmaps and minimize operational impact.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/implement/deprecations-customer-service

## Moderate Changes

- **Send rich messages**

  Removed Apple Messages for Business from the admin-enabled customization table for rich messages, reflecting that it is no longer listed as supported. Admins should update configurations and templates to rely on currently supported channels.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/rich-messages-conversation-control
