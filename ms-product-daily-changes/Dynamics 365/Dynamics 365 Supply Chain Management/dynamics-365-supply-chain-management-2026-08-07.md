## Major Changes

- **Scan bar codes using a camera in the Warehouse Management mobile app**

  Updated guidance for app version 4.1.5 and later to introduce continuous scanning on the camera page with visual outlines and decoded values, replacing manual start/timeout behavior. Added controls for Back, Flashlight, and Accept and clarified behavior when multiple bar codes are detected. Enabled customization of the scan marker (color and thickness) with per-worker/device persistence to improve visibility. Expanded and clarified supported symbologies and platform decoding engines, including DataBar formats, Micro QR, MSI Plessey (iOS), and Micro PDF417, plus platform notes and OS requirements. Included practical tips for lighting, distance, alignment, and handling challenging labels to improve scan reliability.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/warehousing/scan-bar-codes-using-a-camera
