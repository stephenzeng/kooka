## New Articles

- **"The Company Does Not Exist" Canceling a Purchase Order Line**

  New troubleshooting guidance explains how to resolve the “The company does not exist” error when canceling a purchase order line delivery remainder in intercompany scenarios. It identifies the root cause as an incomplete intercompany link and outlines pre-checks and potential impacts before proceeding. The article provides a safe, step-by-step fix using the Disconnect intercompany orders periodic task to remove the broken link so cancellation can succeed. It also cautions against editing intercompany fields directly in the database and links to related intercompany documentation for further context.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/supply-chain/procurement/company-does-not-exist-cancel-po-line-remainder

## Major Changes

- **Migrate to Unified pricing management (preview)**

  Updated the migration guide’s support matrix to confirm that Rebate management is now fully supported across pricing features and migration. This change enables organizations to include rebates when adopting Unified pricing management, reducing gaps and workarounds. It streamlines pricing transitions, improves compatibility, and helps ensure a consistent pricing and rebate experience during and after migration.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/unified-pricing-management/upm-migrate-to-unified-pricing-management
