- **Configure purchase order confirmation batch jobs**

  Introduced guidance for configuring and scheduling the Confirm purchase orders batch job to streamline bulk PO confirmations. Clarifies print behavior (Current vs After), how print management destinations affect batch runs, and when to enable Late selection. Provides best practices for cadence (e.g., avoiding overly frequent recurrences), and shows how to exclude vendors on hold via queries to prevent failures. Includes setup for email delivery and optional printed copies of confirmation journals.
