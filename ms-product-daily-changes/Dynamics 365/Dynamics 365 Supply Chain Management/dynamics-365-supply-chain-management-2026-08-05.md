# Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management
**Date created:** 2026-08-05 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure Supplier Engagement elements in Power Platform (preview)**

  Updated guidance to remove instructions about maintaining localized email templates, reducing outdated or unnecessary steps. Clarified that the invitation email template includes a supplier portal URL placeholder that must be set before go-live to ensure correct links in supplier communications.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/supplier-engagement/deploy-configure-power-platform

- **Configure Supplier Engagement features in Supply Chain Management (preview)**

  Updated workflow configuration steps to align with current UI labels, replacing “Automatic action” with “Auto complete action.” Guidance now specifies selecting Approve or Complete as appropriate and adding a Close step to ensure workflows finish correctly.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/supplier-engagement/deploy-configure-scm
