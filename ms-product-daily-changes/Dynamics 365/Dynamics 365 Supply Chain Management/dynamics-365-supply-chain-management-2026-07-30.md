**Tags:** Administration  

## New Articles

- **Use financial tags in procurement documents**

  Introduced comprehensive guidance for configuring and using financial tags across procurement processes. Explains prerequisites and how to enable the capability in Feature management, then shows how to create and manage up to 20 tags and control behavior at headers vs. lines. Details how tags propagate between documents, when they become read-only during posting, and how workflows and approvals impact editability. Includes step-by-step procedures and behavior matrices for purchase requisitions, purchase orders, purchase agreements, RFQs, and inventory ownership change journals.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/procurement/financial-tags-procurement
