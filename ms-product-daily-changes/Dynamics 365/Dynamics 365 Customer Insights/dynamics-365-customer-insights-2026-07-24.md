# Dynamics 365 Customer Insights
**Date created:** 2026-07-24 UTC  
**Tags:** Other  

## Moderate Changes

- **Email bounce categories**

  Updated bounce category names (for example, “greylisting” to “greylisting-issues” and “routing-errors” to “routing-issues”) and refined descriptions, including clarifying that “full-mailbox” isn’t a hard bounce. Expanded guidance explains DMARC, SPF, and DKIM to help teams diagnose deliverability issues more accurately. Improved accessibility and consistency by switching to the :::image syntax with descriptive alt text.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-insights/journeys/email-bounce-categories
