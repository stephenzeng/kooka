# Dynamics 365 Customer Insights
**Date created:** 2026-09-03 UTC  
**Tags:** Analytics, Compliance, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Exclude bot interactions for reliable analytics**

  Updated guidance to emphasize analytics reliability and clarify that bot protection does not apply to email opens. Removed an outdated note about default enablement for organizations created after July 22, 2024, and streamlined the impact and FAQ sections for clearer interpretation of metrics and configuration choices.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-insights/journeys/bot-protection

- **Customer Insights - Journeys link tracking mechanics**

  Removed an outdated notice about disabling tracking for links in messages older than six months. Clarified how link rewriting and open tracking function, and how consent governs whether interactions are recorded anonymously, improving transparency for analysts and compliance teams.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-insights/journeys/real-time-marketing-link-tracking-mechanics
