## New Articles

- **Tracking consent in Customer Insights - Journeys**

  Introduced guidance for configuring tracking consent using compliance profiles, including region-specific profiles and a Restrictive enforcement model. Clarifies how to add a dedicated tracking consent checkbox to forms and preference centers to control email open and link tracking, UTM parameters, and form prefill. Outlines how to review and import existing consent records via the Consent Center or individual records and recommends informing recipients with links to preference centers. Highlights expected impacts on engagement data, anonymous interactions after opt-out, and propagation delays (up to 24 hours for link/open tracking and about 15 minutes for form prefill).
