# Microsoft 365 Copilot for Sales
**Date created:** 2026-08-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Guidance, Troubleshooting  

## New Articles

- **Reset the Salesforce Server-to-Server Connector in Sales Agent**

  Introduced a step-by-step troubleshooting guide to fully reset the Salesforce server-to-server connector when enable or disable operations fail due to mismatched Salesforce artifacts and Dataverse credentials. The article details prerequisites, important deletion warnings, and clear steps to remove Salesforce artifacts, delete the related Dataverse credential record, and re-enable the connection in admin settings. It also clarifies naming variations in UI labels and links to related error-resolution guidance to streamline recovery.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/sales-copilot/crm-permissions-and-configurations/reset-salesforce-server-to-server-connector

## Moderate Changes

- **Errors when you disable Salesforce with server-to-server flow**

  Updated troubleshooting guidance with a concise Summary section, standardized headings (Resolution renamed to Solution), and clearer cause descriptions. Expanded the Symptoms section with improved formatting and examples, and added a last-resort recommendation to reset the connector with a link to the reset guide.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/sales-copilot/crm-permissions-and-configurations/errors-disabling-salesforce-server-to-server-flow

- **Errors When Enabling Salesforce with Server-to-Server Flow**

  Added a last-resort procedure directing admins to reset the Salesforce connector when artifacts and stored credentials are out of sync. Refined headings and scenario titles and clarified error contexts, including multiple possible messages and license limit explanations, to speed diagnostics.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/sales-copilot/crm-permissions-and-configurations/errors-enabling-salesforce-server-to-server-flow

Adjusted behavior notes to ensure Update from store is shown for older extensions without a recorded source version, improving admin clarity and reducing missed updates.
