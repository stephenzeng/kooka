# Microsoft 365 Copilot for Sales
**Date created:** 2026-07-25 UTC  
**Tags:** Agent  

## Major Changes

- **View customer communication (preview)**

  Added email insights to the Customer communication card alongside meeting insights, extending coverage across descriptions, notes, and usage guidance. Clarified eligibility and data availability for email insights, including a 90-day window and required conditions such as an external CRM contact, S2S connection, email insights enabled, and matching sensitivity labels. Updated prerequisites to include enabling email insights and refined meeting insights setup. Enhanced Outlook guidance to show how to view email and meeting insights together, and clarified when the card appears for emails and meetings tied to opportunities.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-sales-copilot/view-customer-communication

## Moderate Changes

- **View opportunity insights (preview)**

  Expanded Opportunity insights to include emails in addition to meetings across the experience. Clarified when the card appears for each channel and documented a 90-day email insights window with required conditions; added prerequisites to enable Email insights and Email insights sharing.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-sales-copilot/view-opportunity-insights
