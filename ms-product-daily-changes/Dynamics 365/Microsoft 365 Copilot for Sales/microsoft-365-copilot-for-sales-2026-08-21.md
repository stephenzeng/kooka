# Microsoft 365 Copilot for Sales
**Date created:** 2026-08-21 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Guidance  

## Moderate Changes

- **Configure starter prompts for Sales agent chat**

  Added support for displaying starter prompts in two new contexts: Calendar (Outlook or Teams meeting invitations) and Email (Outlook messages or inbox). Clarified prompt tagging by distinguishing out-of-the-box prompts as Default and custom prompts as Custom, improving governance and discoverability. Introduced a limit of 50 custom prompts across all contexts to help admins plan prompt management.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-sales-copilot/starter-prompts
