# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-19 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Deprecation, Guidance, Security  

## New Articles

- **Adjust inventory levels**

  Introduced a how-to guide for adjusting product inventory at a warehouse, covering scenarios like initial stock, shrinkage/defects, and discrepancy corrections. Explains how inventory journals update Quantity on Hand and, in turn, Quantity Available, with notes on when to use manual journals for advanced needs. Provides prerequisites and step-by-step instructions, including adding positive or negative quantities, the immutability of the warehouse once products are added, and allowance for negative on-hand quantities. Includes verification steps and links to related next steps.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/adjust-inventory

- **Transfer inventory between warehouses**

  Added guidance for transferring inventory between warehouses, detailing prerequisites and how inventory journals affect Quantity on Hand and Quantity Available. Walks through creating a transfer, adding products, and verifying balances at both source and destination warehouses. Includes steps to enable inventory transfers in the Field Service mobile app by configuring tables and offline profiles, plus links to related next steps.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/transfer-inventory

## Major Changes

- **Access related apps from the mobile app**

  Added a deprecation notice to alert readers to changes affecting Dynamics 365 Remote Assist and Dynamics 365 Guides when accessing related apps from the Field Service mobile app. This clarifies future availability and helps organizations plan transitions or replacements accordingly. Other edits were minor metadata and formatting updates.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/mobile/access-related-apps

## Moderate Changes
