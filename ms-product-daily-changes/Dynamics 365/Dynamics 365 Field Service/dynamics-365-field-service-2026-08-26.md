# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-26 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Guidance, Troubleshooting  

## Moderate Changes

- **Known issues and limitations**

  Removed a previously listed limitation stating that actuals from the integration aren’t associated with the originating work order. This reflects a behavior and documentation update, improving accuracy and traceability for integrated actuals.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/project-operations-integration-issues

- **Troubleshoot Work Order Save or Creation Errors**

  Expanded troubleshooting coverage with new scenarios and clearer steps, including guidance for conditional required fields (such as price lists and currency alignment) and detailed incident type behavior. Enhanced issue diagnostics for symptoms like duplicate child records, unexpected primary incidents, and status changes, and clarified timing considerations for plug-ins and business rules with steps to isolate custom logic. The article title and description were updated to reflect its broader scope.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/field-service/work-order/work-order-creation-errors
