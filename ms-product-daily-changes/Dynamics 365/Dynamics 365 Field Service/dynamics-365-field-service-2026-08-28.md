# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-28 UTC  
**Tags:** Configuration, Troubleshooting  

## New Articles

- **Troubleshoot Dynamics 365 Field Service Settings Issues**

  Introduced a comprehensive troubleshooting guide for common Field Service settings problems. Covers resolving “database version is out of date” after solution updates, ensuring settings take effect (including when app or sitemap changes must be published), and configuring auto-numbering for key entities with a one-way opt-in. Clarifies required permissions, that the settings record can’t be deleted, and addresses tax calculation discrepancies across environments. Provides step-by-step procedures and links to related references to speed resolution.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/field-service/administration/settings-troubleshooting

## Major Changes

- **Troubleshoot Common Mobile App Issues and Errors**

  Updated guidance to support Microsoft Entra B2B collaborators signing in to the Field Service mobile app by switching to their guest tenant. Added instructions for using a formatted deep link to access the guest tenant directly. Removed obsolete recommendations that the app doesn’t support guest accounts or that customers should create internal accounts or use a portal, reducing friction for cross-tenant access.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/field-service/mobile-app/mobile-app-common-issues
