# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-06 UTC  
**Tags:** Administration, Other  

## Moderate Changes

- **Create work order templates with incident types**

  Updated guidance clarifies that detailing products, services, service tasks, and characteristics in an incident type automatically populates corresponding work order details and explains how estimated duration is derived. A new HVAC Annual Inspection example demonstrates auto-creation of related items and duration calculation. Added prerequisites to ensure auto-population works reliably, including correct product status, comprehensive price list coverage, and required create privileges.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/configure-incident-types

- **Overview of Dynamics 365 Field Service**

  Introduced a new service delivery models section that explains reactive, preventive, predictive (IoT), and project-based approaches, helping organizations choose the best fit or mix. Added a note connecting the service-to-cash flow with the work order lifecycle and system statuses to clarify end-to-end process context.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/overview
