# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-08-29 UTC  
**Tags:** Compliance, Configuration, Governance, Guidance  

## New Articles

- **Dynamics 365 Field Service in US government clouds**

  Introduced a new page that outlines Field Service availability and capability support across US government clouds (GCC, GCC High, DoD). Provides a feature matrix showing supported capabilities, including core Field Service, Connected Field Service, and Field Service mobile apps, and confirms Universal Resource Scheduling availability. Clearly identifies unavailable capabilities such as Resource Scheduling Optimization, Scheduling Operations Agent, Field Service Rentals, Field Service and Project Operations integration, Field Service Portal, Field Service Custom Controls, and Universal Scheduling Service. Helps government customers set expectations, plan deployments, and find related context via the Dynamics 365 US Government overview.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/field-service-government

## Moderate Changes

- **Customize the Field Service mobile app**

  Expanded and reorganized customization guidance into task-based sections for common scenarios. Added detailed instructions for configuring app modules, forms, views, navigation, dynamic font scaling, deep links, Power Apps configuration, and implementing custom controls (booking status icons, note-taking, mobile lookup, disabling lookup clickthrough, and expanding list/subgrid data). This improves discoverability and gives admins step-by-step guidance to tailor the mobile experience.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/mobile/customize-mobile-app
