# Dynamics 365 Field Service
**Date created:** 2026-07-24 UTC  
**Tags:** Automation  

## Moderate Changes

- **Run an optimization ad hoc**

  Added an “Interpret optimization results” section with a troubleshooting table to explain outcomes after running schedule optimization. It clarifies why requirements may remain unscheduled or be excluded, how constraints drive resource/time selection, the effect of locked bookings, and how to address failed or canceled requests, with links to related guidance. This improves diagnosis and resolution of optimization issues, reducing trial-and-error.

  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/rso-run-optimization
